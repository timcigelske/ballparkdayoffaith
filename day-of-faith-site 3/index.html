Our venue. Our community.

Across from Lambeau Field.

Lambeau Leap of Faith brings together families, parishes, friends and football fans for a day that celebrates what matters most: faith, family and fellowship.

The event identity uses a simple, IP-safe sports motif: red energy on one side, blue and gold on the other, a football between them, and the message of faith, football and fellowship at the center.
