What to expect

A day that is holy—and fun.

Mass

Open-air Sunday Mass in the parking lot, surrounded by friends, families and the joyful energy of game day.

Tailgate

Food, fellowship and hospitality before first pitch. As the incense goes down, the smoke from the brats comes up.

Game

Cheer together at American Family Field. Ticket options and pricing will be posted when the 2027 date is finalized.

Location

American Family Field

Ballpark Day of Faith takes place in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Mass and the tailgate are traditionally held in the Uecker parking lot, rain or shine.
