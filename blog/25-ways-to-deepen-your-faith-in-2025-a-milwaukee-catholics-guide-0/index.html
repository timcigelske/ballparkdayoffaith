10 Reasons to Attend Ballpark Day of Faith

Ballpark Day of Faith brings faith, fellowship and baseball together at American Family Field in Milwaukee. The experience has become a beloved tradition for families and friends. Here are ten compelling reasons to attend. 

 

  1. Open-Air Mass: Begin the day with a spiritually enriching open-air Mass at 10:15 am in the Uecker lot of American Family Field.

  2. Tailgate Lunch: Enjoy a traditional tailgate lunch, featuring classic ballpark fare and beverages, fostering community and camaraderie among attendees.

  3. Milwaukee Brewers Game: Cheer on the Milwaukee Brewers as they face the St. Louis Cardinals, with the first pitch at 1:10 pm. Tickets on sale soon! 

  4. Family-Friendly Activities: The event is designed for families, offering activities suitable for all ages, making it an ideal outing for parents and children alike.

  5. Community Building: Connect with fellow attendees from various backgrounds, strengthening communal bonds and sharing in a collective expression of faith.

  6. Support for Non-Profits: Participate in an event that collaborates with local non-profits, providing opportunities to support charitable causes within the community.

  7. Unity: The event welcomes individuals of all faiths and backgrounds, promoting inclusivity and unity. 

  8. Affordable Entertainment: With reasonably priced tickets that often include the game and tailgate, it offers an economical option for a full day of entertainment. You can also help a neighbor in need! If you would like to support us in sending those who otherwise would not be able to go, we offer an option to donate a ticket. This includes a Brewers ticket, transportation and a meal.

  9. Unique Experience: Combining religious observance with America's favorite pastime provides a distinctive and memorable experience for attendees.

  10. Tradition: Join a growing tradition that has been bringing people together for over a decade, celebrating faith, family and fellowship year after year!

Attending the Ballpark Day of Faith offers a unique opportunity to engage in spiritual reflection, enjoy communal activities, and partake in a beloved sporting event, all within a single day. Join the experience!

Keep the faith and fellowship going.
