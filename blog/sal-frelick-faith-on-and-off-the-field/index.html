Sal Frelick: Faith On and Off the Field

Apr 5, 2025, 11:46:39 AM


Sal Freelick playing at Boston College, a Jesuit university. Photo by Bryan Green. 

Sal Frelick has emerged not only as a promising outfielder for the Brewers but also as a beacon of Catholic faith and values. His journey from the pews of St. Brigid Parish in Lexington, Massachusetts, to Major League Baseball is a narrative interwoven with devotion, discipline, and a deep-rooted commitment to his beliefs.​

Early Foundations in Faith

Growing up in Lexington, Frelick's life was steeped in Catholic traditions. He served as an altar boy for a decade at St. Brigid Parish, an experience that instilled in him a profound sense of service and community. This early involvement laid the groundwork for a faith that would guide him through the challenges and triumphs of his athletic career. ​

Frelick's educational journey began at St. Agnes School in Arlington, Massachusetts, where he attended from kindergarten through third or fourth grade. This Catholic educational environment further nurtured his developing faith. He later transitioned to public schooling in Lexington, where his athletic talents flourished. Excelling in football, hockey, and baseball, Frelick was named the Gatorade State Player of the Year in football during his senior year, a testament to his multifaceted abilities. ​

When it came time to choose a college, Frelick's faith played a pivotal role. He opted for Boston College, a Jesuit institution known for its commitment to both academic excellence and spiritual development. Reflecting on his decision, Frelick emphasized the importance of spreading God's word and being a disciple, values that resonated with the Jesuit mission of the college. 

“Faith has always been a very important part of my life. It has played a big role in my athletic life, where I chose to go to school, and most importantly my family life,” Frelick said. 

Faith in Professional Life


Frelick can be seen on the field wearing his crucifix 

Frelick's ascent to professional baseball did not diminish his commitment to his faith. Upon joining the Milwaukee Brewers, he continued to embody the principles instilled in him during his formative years. Notably, the morning after the Brewers clinched the National League Central Division championship in mid-September 2024, instead of indulging in rest after the celebrations, Frelick chose to visit children at a local hospital.

"As a Catholic, it comes back to being a good human being," he told the Milwaukee Catholic Herald. "I love giving back to the community, and as a kid, I looked up to players who did that." ​

On the field, Frelick is often seen wearing a crucifix beneath his uniform, a subtle yet powerful testament to his enduring faith. He expressed a desire for people to recognize this symbol. 

"I want people to see I’m wearing it," he said. "I really want people to know that I have faith." ​

A Role Model for the Community

Frelick's commitment extends beyond symbolic gestures. He actively engages with various community initiatives, particularly those aligned with his faith-driven values. His involvement with the Lexington High School unified basketball team, where he has provided support and resources, underscores his dedication to inclusivity and service. Such actions reflect the core Catholic tenet of aiding those in need and fostering a sense of community. ​

Sal Frelick's journey is a compelling narrative of how faith can seamlessly integrate with professional aspirations. His life serves as an inspiring example for young athletes and the broader community, illustrating that success is not solely measured by accolades but also by the positive impact one has on others. In Frelick, the Milwaukee Brewers and their fans have not only a talented player but also a role model whose faith and actions enrich the community both on and off the field.​
