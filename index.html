Welcome to the party

What is Ballpark Day of Faith?

It began at a tailgater. A few friends gathered around a Smokey Joe over some beers, sharing their love of sports, family and faith. Each year, the party kept getting bigger.

Today, families and friends gather for Sunday Mass and a tailgate before the game. Bring your family. Invite a friend. All are welcome.
